# Bus Routes and Timetable

## Routes
The campus shuttle runs between LNMIIT and two city points:
- LNMIIT ↔ Raja Park
- LNMIIT ↔ Ajmeri Gate

## Two timetables
- **Weekday timetable** runs Monday–Friday. Some services run on a single weekday
  only (for example a Monday-only or Friday-only trip).
- **Weekend/holiday timetable** runs on Saturdays, Sundays, and any date the
  transport office marks as a holiday.

## Sample weekday services
- Bus 1: LNMIIT → Raja Park at 06:00; Raja Park → LNMIIT at 07:00.
- Bus 2: LNMIIT → Ajmeri Gate at 07:00; Ajmeri Gate → LNMIIT at 08:00.
- Bus 4: LNMIIT → Raja Park at 10:00; Raja Park → LNMIIT at 11:00.
- Evening: LNMIIT → Ajmeri Gate at 18:05 and 18:45; return services around 20:15–21:00.

## Sample weekend/holiday services
- Bus 1: LNMIIT → Ajmeri Gate at 07:00; Ajmeri Gate → LNMIIT at 08:00.
- Bus 2: LNMIIT → Raja Park at 10:00; Raja Park → LNMIIT at 12:00.
- Afternoon/evening services to Raja Park and Ajmeri Gate through ~21:00.

## Capacity
- Buses seat 40 by default; the transport office can adjust capacity per service.

Always check the live schedule in the app for the exact trips and seat availability
on a given date — these are representative times, and holidays shift the timetable.

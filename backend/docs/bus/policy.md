# Campus Bus Booking Policy

## Who can use the bus service
- The campus bus service is free and available only to institute accounts (an
  `@lnmiit.ac.in` email) that have a roll number set on their profile.
- Each rider may hold at most one seat per trip.

## Booking lifecycle and timing
Every trip moves through timed phases relative to its departure time:
- **Booking opens 60 minutes before departure.** Before that the trip is "scheduled"
  and not yet bookable.
- **Confirmation window opens 20 minutes before departure.** From this point, riders
  who booked must confirm they are boarding ("confirm to board").
- **Auto-release sweep runs 10 minutes before departure.** Any seat still only
  "assigned" (booked but not confirmed) is released and offered to the waitlist.
- **At departure** the trip closes; anyone still unconfirmed is marked a no-show.

## Confirming your seat (confirm to board)
- Booking a seat during the confirmation window confirms it immediately.
- Booking earlier assigns the seat; you must return during the confirmation window
  (from 20 minutes before departure) and confirm, or the seat is auto-released.

## Waitlist
- When a trip is full, riders join a public first-in-first-out (FIFO) waitlist.
- Freed seats are offered to the waitlist in join order before any new direct
  booking — if anyone is waiting, direct booking is refused and you must join the
  waitlist.
- Promotions during or after the confirmation window are confirmed automatically.

## Cancelling and declining
- You can cancel an assigned seat any time before the trip closes; the seat is
  freed and offered to the waitlist.
- Declining during the confirmation window is a responsible action (frees the seat
  for someone else) and is **not** counted as a no-show.

## No-shows and reliability
- A **no-show** means you booked a seat but never boarded (never confirmed, or the
  seat was auto-released). No-shows are counted on your profile.
- "Declined" and "cancelled" are responsible behaviours and are not no-shows.
- The transport office periodically reviews reliability; repeated no-shows —
  especially when they block waitlisted riders — may lead to a warning or a
  temporary booking cooldown. These reviews are advisory; staff make the decision.

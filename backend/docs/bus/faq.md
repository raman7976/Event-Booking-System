# Bus Service FAQ

**Q: How early can I book a seat?**
Booking opens 60 minutes before a trip's departure time. Before that the trip shows
as scheduled and isn't bookable yet.

**Q: I booked but forgot to confirm — what happens?**
If you don't confirm during the confirmation window (from 20 minutes before
departure), your seat is auto-released about 10 minutes before departure and given
to the next person on the waitlist. An unconfirmed seat counts as a no-show.

**Q: The bus is full. Can I still get on?**
Join the waitlist. It's first-in-first-out — when someone cancels or is
auto-released, the freed seat goes to the person who has been waiting longest.

**Q: Does cancelling count against me?**
No. Cancelling or declining frees your seat for someone else and is treated as
responsible behaviour — it is not a no-show. Only booking and then never boarding
counts as a no-show.

**Q: Why can't I use the bus service?**
The bus service needs an institute account (`@lnmiit.ac.in`) with a roll number set
on your profile. Add your roll number in your profile settings.

**Q: How many seats can I book?**
One seat per trip.

**Q: Is there a charge?**
No — the campus bus service is free.

**Q: Which routes run on Sunday?**
Sundays use the weekend/holiday timetable (for example LNMIIT ↔ Ajmeri Gate and
LNMIIT ↔ Raja Park services). Check the live schedule for the exact times.
